What is CollegePast?

CollegePast is a simple platform designed to help College of Education students find and access past examination questions quickly.

Questions are organized using a clear path:

Level→Course/Paper→Academic Year→Semester→Mentoring University
How it works

Six simple steps

01

Select your level

Choose Level 100, 200, 300 or 400.

02

Select your course

Pick the course or paper you want.

03

Select the year

Choose the academic year.

04

Select the semester

Choose First or Second Semester.

05

Select your university

Choose your mentoring university.

06

View or download

Open the PDF or save it for later.
